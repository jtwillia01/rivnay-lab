In addition, Josh was named NU Postdoctoral Association’s Postdoc of the month for the month of August 2022: [https://sites.northwestern.edu/nupa/postdoc-of-the-month-1/](https://sites.northwestern.edu/nupa/postdoc-of-the-month-1/)
